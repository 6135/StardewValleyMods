# Changelog

## 1.0.1
- Added Ultimate Fertilizer compatibility: the fertilizer works when stacked with others, and only it is removed when the giant crop forms.

## 1.0.0
- Port of atravita's Giant Crop Fertilizer to Stardew Valley 1.6.15, without AtraCore or Json Assets.
- New item ID `6135.GiantCropFertilizer_Fertilizer`; the fertilizer works anywhere in a giant crop's footprint and is consumed when the giant crop grows.
- Added a crafting recipe (1 Iridium Bar + 3 Quality Fertilizer → 1), learned at Farming level 10 or bought early from Mr. Qi's gem shop for 100 Qi gems.
